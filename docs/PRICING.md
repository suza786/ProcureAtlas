# Commercial Model — ProcureAtlas AI

**Status:** Draft. Pricing validation required before launch (see `KNOWN_RISKS.md`).
**Last updated:** 2026-09-30

This document defines the planned commercial model for ProcureAtlas AI. Pricing is structured per region (Bangladesh and International) to reflect purchasing-power parity and market maturity. All prices below are **planning figures** — they require validation through customer discovery interviews before launch.

---

## 1. Pricing Principles

1. **Free tier in every region** — frictionless entry; lets a prospective customer experience the product before paying.
2. **Value-based tiering** — higher tiers unlock more pursuits, more tracked tenders, more seats, more API access.
3. **Purchasing-power parity** — Bangladesh pricing reflects local purchasing power; international pricing reflects global SaaS benchmarks.
4. **No surprise overage** — soft caps with clear upgrade prompts; no auto-billing for overage without consent.
5. **Annual discount** — ~2 months free on annual prepay (standard SaaS convention).
6. **Enterprise is custom** — procurement, security review, custom integrations, on-prem options, dedicated support.

## 5. Usage Metering + Overage

- **Soft caps** — when a tenant approaches 80% / 95% / 100% of any quota, in-app + email notifications prompt upgrade.
- **No hard cut-off at 100%** — the tenant can keep working; overage tracked for the billing cycle.
- **Overage billing** — at the end of each cycle, if the tenant exceeded quota by > 10%, the system prompts upgrade. No auto-charge for overage without tenant opt-in.
- **Annual true-up** — for Enterprise tiers, annual true-up adjusts the next renewal based on actual peak usage.

---

## 6. Annual Discount

- Annual prepay: 2 months free (~17% discount) on all paid tiers.
- Multi-year: additional 5% per year (capped at 15% for 3-year prepay).

---

## 7. Free Trial of Paid Tiers

- 14-day free trial of Professional (BD) / Pursuit Pro (Intl) on sign-up, no credit card required.
- Trial converts to Free tier on expiry (not auto-billed).
- In-app upgrade prompts at any time during the trial.

---

## 8. Refund + Cancellation Policy

- Monthly subscriptions: cancel anytime; access continues to end of billing period; no prorated refund.
- Annual subscriptions: cancel anytime; access continues to end of prepaid period; no refund except where required by law.
- Enterprise: per contract terms.

## 9. Validation Notes — Required Before Launch

The pricing in this document is a planning draft. Before launch, the following validation is required (see `KNOWN_RISKS.md`):

1. **Customer discovery interviews** — at least 10 interviews per region (Bangladesh, International) with target personas (Bid Manager, BD Lead, SME Owner) to test willingness-to-pay.
2. **Competitive pricing benchmark** — refresh `COMPETITOR_MATRIX.md` pricing data; verify ProcureAtlas AI pricing is defensibly positioned.
3. **Unit economics test** — confirm that Solo / Intelligence tier covers per-tenant infrastructure cost at expected usage; confirm Professional / Pursuit Pro tier covers CSM cost.
4. **Bangladesh pricing sanity-check** — verify ৳2,990 / ৳9,900 / ৳29,900 are psychologically acceptable price points (not too close, not too far apart).
5. **Currency volatility review** — Bangladesh Taka to USD volatility may affect international cost-of-goods (LLM API, cloud infra); review quarterly (see `KNOWN_RISKS.md`).
6. **Tax / VAT consideration** — Bangladesh VAT (15%) on SaaS; EU VAT on EU customers; US sales tax nexus — billing system must handle.
